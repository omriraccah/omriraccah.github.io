## About me
***

I am a cognitive neuroscientist. I study how we form memories from complex experiences. My research focuses on the following questions:

- How does the brain combine multiple senses into a single, integrated memory?
- How does the episodic memory system support the integration, representation, and retrieval of structured sequences?

I am currently a postdoctoral fellow at Yale University working with [Nicholas Turk-Browne](https://ntblab.yale.edu/){:target="_blank"}, supported by an NIH NRSA fellowship. I completed my Ph.D. in 2023 at New York University with [David Poeppel](https://as.nyu.edu/faculty/david-poeppel.html){:target="_blank"}.
